# Portfolio Case Study Assignment

## Why it matters

A portfolio is mostly the framing around the work, and the framing is what makes a stranger trust you or scroll past. AI means you never face a blank page again, but only if you use it to get your decisions and results out, not to borrow a generic story.

## Requirements

1. Write a voice card, five to seven words for how you want to sound.
2. Be interviewed about each piece of work.
3. Ask one question at a time until the problem, decisions, and outcome are clear.
4. Draft each piece into a case study with three beats:
   - the problem
   - what I did and what I decided
   - what came of it
5. Include bio and contact/CTA copy.
6. Direct AI to use my words and keep my voice.
7. Edit hard. Remove anything I wouldn't say or can't stand behind.

## Deliverable

One document containing the framed cases for every piece the sitemap calls for, with the voice card at the top, plus one before/after example:
- generic AI line
- edited personal version

## Pass criteria

- A framed case exists for every required piece.
- No case is just a screenshot or title.
- Every case has the three beats.
- Each case describes that specific project.
- The writing sounds like a specific person.
- Before/after demonstrates the difference from generic AI copy.
- It speaks to one audience and points to one action.
- Keep it tight.
- Avoid "results-driven" and similar generic language.
